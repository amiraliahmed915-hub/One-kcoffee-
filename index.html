Our Story

Rooted in Ethiopia's Coffee Heritage

Ethiopia is the birthplace of coffee — and at One-K Coffee we are proud to carry that tradition into every cup. From the first aroma to the last sip, everything we serve is prepared with care, patience, and a genuine love for the craft.

Whether you're catching up with friends, taking a quiet moment for yourself, or powering through a busy day in Addis Ababa, One-K is a warm place to slow down and enjoy the little ritual that makes everything better.

  • Quality Ethiopian BeansCarefully selected for a rich, honest cup.
  • Handcrafted DrinksFrom espresso classics to traditional jebena buna.
  • A Warm WelcomeA cozy space for conversation, work, and relaxation.
